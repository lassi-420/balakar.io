Captions in the language your audience speaks.

A free After Effects panel that turns speech into styled captions in Hindi, Urdu, Tamil and 17 more languages. It runs on your own PC, so there is no account to make and nothing to pay for.

Install in three steps.

  1. Download the installerOne file, Balakar-Setup.exe, from the button above or the Releases page.
  2. Run it and pick a speech modelSmall is a good start. Setup downloads Python, the AI libraries, ffmpeg and your model once. Allow 10 to 30 minutes and a few GB of space.
  3. Restart After EffectsOpen Window then Extensions then Balakar. Select a clip, press Transcribe.

Your footage never leaves your PC.

Speech recognition, translation and audio cleanup all run locally. After setup you can unplug the network and keep working.

The engine listens only on 127.0.0.1 and needs a secret token that changes every time it starts, so other programs and web pages cannot talk to it.

The whole project is MIT-licensed. Read it, change it, ship your own build.

Questions people ask.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”.

The installer is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen warns about any new unsigned file. Choose More info, then Run anyway. You can check the file against the SHA-256 listed on the release page, or build it yourself from the source.

Do I need a graphics card?

No. The Small model works on a normal CPU. An NVIDIA card makes transcription much faster and lets you use the Large v3 turbo model comfortably.

Which After Effects versions work?

Any version that supports CEP panels (2020 and later). The installer turns on the per-user setting that lets After Effects load unsigned panels.

What about Mac and Linux?

The installer is Windows-only for now. The engine and panel are plain Python and JavaScript, so a Mac build is a good first contribution.

Urdu or Arabic letters look disconnected.

In After Effects, open Preferences, Type, and set Text Engine to South Asian and Middle Eastern. Also pick a font that supports the script.

Can I use my own fine-tuned Whisper model?

Yes. Add its folder under model_overrides in engine/config.json, one entry per language. This is how Odia and Kashmiri are supported.

How do I uninstall?

Settings, Apps, Balakar, Uninstall. It removes the panel, the engine, the models and the startup entry.
